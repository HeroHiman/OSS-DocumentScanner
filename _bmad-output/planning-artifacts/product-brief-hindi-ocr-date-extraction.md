# Product Brief: Hindi OCR & Date Extraction Feature

## 1. Objective
Integrate Google ML Kit (Devanagari model) to accurately parse dates from handwritten and printed Hindi/Devanagari documents, and provide an intuitive, editable UI for manual corrections and chronological sorting.

## 2. Business Context
Crucial for digitizing daily handwritten ledgers, mandi receipts, and supplier invoices (e.g. khova, milk, grains, and namkeen material bills) without relying on slow manual data entry.

## 3. Success Metrics
- **OCR processing latency**: Under 500ms per page on standard Android hardware.
- **Memory Safety**: Zero Out-Of-Memory (OOM) crashes during 50-page batch processing.
- **Accuracy**: Date extraction accuracy exceeding 85% for standard Hinglish and Devanagari date formats.
